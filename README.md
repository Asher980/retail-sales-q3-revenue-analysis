# Retail Sales Analysis: Investigating Q3 Revenue Decline

## Project Overview

This project explores a retail sales transaction dataset to investigate a business concern about declining revenue in the third quarter (Q3).

Management initially suspected that the decline was caused by a company-wide problem. Rather than accepting this assumption, I used data analysis to investigate the relationship between revenue, order volume, average quantity per order, and average order value.

The analysis revealed that although Q3 recorded the highest number of orders, it had the lowest revenue, average quantity per order, and average order value.

This demonstrated the importance of investigating business problems beyond their initial assumptions.

## Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare the retail sales dataset for analysis.
- Examine missing and questionable data and make responsible cleaning decisions.
- Analyze revenue performance across quarters.
- Compare quarterly revenue with the number of orders.
- Investigate average quantity per order and average order value.
- Identify factors contributing to the Q3 revenue decline.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Data Cleaning

Data cleaning was an important part of this project.

The process involved evaluating missing and questionable records and considering whether to retain or remove them based on their potential impact on the analysis.

The objective was not simply to produce a clean-looking dataset, but to preserve data integrity and avoid introducing bias through inappropriate cleaning decisions.

## Key Findings

### 1. Q3 Recorded the Lowest Revenue
The quarterly revenue analysis showed that Q3 had the lowest revenue compared with the other quarters.

### 2. Q3 Had the Highest Number of Orders
Despite recording the lowest revenue, Q3 had the highest order volume.

### 3. Lowest Average Quantity per Order
Customers purchased fewer quantities per order on average during Q3.

### 4. Lowest Average Order Value
Q3 also recorded the lowest average order value, indicating lower spending per order.

## Business Insights and Recommendations

The findings suggested that the Q3 revenue decline was not necessarily caused by a reduction in customer orders.

Instead, the lower average quantity per order and average order value pointed towards an opportunity to improve the value generated from each transaction.

Recommended areas of focus include:

- Encouraging customers to purchase more items per transaction.
- Exploring product bundling and cross-selling opportunities.
- Reviewing pricing and promotional strategies.
- Monitoring average order value and average quantity per order alongside order volume.

## Conclusion
